# The business side: the next yes, deals, money in

One job: win the next paid yes, and keep the record of each deal and the money from it. Two parts, one folder each.

## Layout
- `the-next-yes/` - who you help, the one line, the work and proof behind it, the people, the platform listings and funders, and every pitch you make. Start at `START-HERE.md`.
- `finances/` - the tracker. One folder per deal (`<payer>/<engagement>/`), one `ledger.csv` of money events, one `quote-history.csv` of trimmed past-job summaries, `rates.md`, `templates/`, and `quote-skill/`. Rules in `finances/CONTEXT.md`.
- `GLOSSARY.md` - what every folder, file and term means.
- `index.html` - the viewer and editor page.

## Does not hold
- Job applications and employment: keep those somewhere else. A role becomes an engagement here only once there is a signed contract.
- Notes about a client's business: keep them in a notes folder of your own, with the same name as the deal, and link both ways.
- Personal money.

## Rules
- `AGENTS.md` governs what assistants may open and what public material may show.
- Each claim is labelled for status and for use (see the README).
- Business money only.
- Assistants draft; you send and publish.
- Existing files are not edited or moved without the owner's OK.

## If unsure where context lives
Stop and ask the owner, then add the answer here.
